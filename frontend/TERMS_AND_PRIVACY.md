# Part I: Terms and Conditions of Use

### 1. Acceptance of Terms & Guardian Consent
By accessing, browsing, downloading, or using **BhashaSetu** ("the Platform", "we", "us", or "our"), including the Web Portal, Android Application, and API services, you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions.

- **For Students (Minors under 18):** If you are a student or child under 18 years of age, you may only use BhashaSetu with the involvement, knowledge, and verifiable consent of a parent, legal guardian, teacher, or authorized school administrator.
- **For Parents, Guardians & Teachers:** By permitting a child to use BhashaSetu, you confirm that you have reviewed and consented to these Terms and the Privacy Policy on their behalf and accept responsibility for their usage.

### 2. Educational Purpose & Hackathon Scope
BhashaSetu is an educational research and assistive technology prototype developed for the **Smart India Hackathon (SIH 2026)** to address learning poverty and language barriers in primary schools across tribal and regional communities (such as Jharkhand and Eastern India).
- The platform is offered strictly for **educational, non-profit, and pedagogical purposes**.
- BhashaSetu provides language translation, phonetic guidance, interactive story reading, and foundational literacy/numeracy tools aligned with the **National Education Policy (NEP 2020)**.

### 3. Child Safety & Acceptable Use Policy
BhashaSetu is committed to maintaining a gentle, positive, and strictly safe learning sanctuary for children. Users and educators agree to:
- **Use for Positive Learning Only:** Use the platform only for classroom education, language learning, and interactive practice.
- **Prohibited Conduct:**
  - Do not input, upload, or transmit any abusive, harmful, vulgar, sexually explicit, defamatory, or threatening language or media.
  - Do not attempt to reverse-engineer, disrupt, or exploit the platform's translation APIs or security safeguards.
  - Do not attempt to solicit or extract personal information from any other student or user.
  - Automated content safety filters and Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) moderation actively block and discard inappropriate inputs.

### 4. AI Pedagogy & Translation Disclaimer
- **Pedagogical Metaphors:** BhashaSetu leverages AI and rule-based vernacular linguistic engines to translate and contextualize primary education content into low-resource indigenous languages (e.g., Santhali, Mundari, Ho, Kurukh, Kharia, Khortha, Nagpuri, Panchpargania, Kurmali).
- **Advisory on Accuracy:** While our linguistic models are curated with linguistic benchmarks and verified by Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) language experts, automated vernacular translations may occasionally contain dialectal variations or minor phonetic discrepancies. BhashaSetu is designed as an instructional companion to teachers, not a replacement for formal classroom educators.

### 5. Intellectual Property & Indigenous Language Rights
- **Cultural Respect & Preservation:** BhashaSetu honors and respects the linguistic heritage and indigenous cultural identities of tribal communities. Native scripts (such as Ol Chiki, Warang Citi, and Tolong Siki) and cultural narratives belong to their respective heritage communities.
- **Platform Code & Software:** The source code, software architecture, UI components, custom character art ("Kuku AI", "Setu Bhaiya"), and pedagogical engines are protected under open-source and hackathon intellectual property provisions.

### 6. Account Credentials & Picture Passwords
- To ensure young learners (ages 5–10) are not burdened with complex email passwords, BhashaSetu provides **kid-friendly identifiers** such as "Magic Words", "Student First Names", and "Picture Passwords" (e.g., matching animal/fruit icons).
- These identifiers are intended for classroom group assignments and device-level personalization and should not contain sensitive government identification numbers (Aadhaar, passport, etc.).

### 7. Offline-First & Service Availability
- BhashaSetu is engineered as an **offline-first platform** to support rural and remote schools with intermittent internet connectivity. Core translation dictionaries, pre-cached learning packs, and speech synthesizers operate locally on devices.
- Cloud-connected features (such as real-time classroom duplex WebSocket streaming) depend on network availability and are provided on an "as-available" basis without guarantees of uninterrupted service.

### 8. Termination & Policy Updates
- We reserve the right to suspend or restrict access to any user or client that violates child-safety rules or attempts malicious access.
- We may periodically refine these Terms to align with evolving regulatory standards or SIH 2026 deployment guidelines. Significant changes will be clearly highlighted on the platform's header.

# Part II: Privacy Policy

### 1. Our Core Privacy Principles
Our privacy architecture is built around **four foundational promises**:
1. **Zero Profiling:** We never build behavioral, commercial, or advertising profiles of children.
2. **Zero Commercial Monetization:** Children's learning activities, voice inputs, and progress are never sold to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.
3. **Data Minimization:** We only collect the minimal, non-identifiable technical data strictly necessary to deliver vernacular learning.
4. **Offline Local Storage:** Wherever feasible, student learning progress stays on the local device or school tablet rather than being centralized on remote servers.

---

### 2. Information We Collect (And What We Never Collect)

#### What We NEVER Collect:
- ❌ Government-issued identification numbers (e.g., Aadhaar, SSN).
- ❌ Precise real-time GPS location tracking.
- ❌ Child biometric facial recognition data.
- ❌ Phone numbers or personal social media credentials of children.
- ❌ Payment or financial billing information.

#### What We May Collect (Minimal Operational Data):
| Data Type | Description | Purpose | Retention |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Student Nickname / Magic Word** | e.g., "Aarav" or "Birsa" (No last name required) | Displaying personalized greetings on the dashboard | Stored locally in browser `localStorage` or local SQLite |
| **Grade Band** | Pre-K, Grades 1–3, or Grades 4–5 | Delivering age-appropriate vocabulary and story levels | Device session level |
| **Language Preference** | e.g., Santhali, Mundari, Hindi, Bengali | Setting default vernacular script and speech voice | Device setting |
| **Learning Quiz Badges** | Quiz score counts and unlocked stickers | Encouraging student achievement and progress | Local device database |
| **Crash & Technical Logs** | Non-identifiable error codes and device OS version | Diagnosing bugs and ensuring low-end tablet stability | Temporary aggregate logs |

---

### 3. Voice, Audio & Speech Data Handling
BhashaSetu features voice assistance and speech playback to help pre-literate children hear correct vernacular pronunciation:
- **Speech Synthesis (Text-to-Speech):** Generated using client-side Web Audio synthesis and local on-device voice libraries. No voice recording leaves the device for standard playback.
- **Speech Input (Microphone Access):** When a student or teacher taps the microphone icon to practice speaking a word, the microphone stream is processed in temporary memory solely to perform speech-to-text conversion.
- **No Voice Fingerprinting:** We do **not** record, store, or analyze children's voiceprints for biometric identification or speaker recognition. Audio streams are discarded immediately following translation synthesis.

---

### 4. How We Use Information
Any collected data is used exclusively to:
- Render translated educational text into native scripts (Devanagari, Ol Chiki, Warang Citi, Bengali, Tamil, etc.).
- Provide real-time peer translation in collaborative classroom modules.
- Allow students to resume reading stories where they left off.
- Improve low-resource tribal language vocabulary coverage through anonymized, aggregated translation feedback verified by linguists.

---

### 5. Data Storage, Retention & Offline Sync
- **Local Storage by Default:** In the Android application and Web client, student progress, bookmarks, and dictionary history are stored in local storage (`SQLite` / `Room` on Android; `IndexedDB` / `localStorage` on Web).
- **Delta Sync:** When connected to school Wi-Fi or networks, the platform performs compressed delta synchronization of verified linguistic packs. No personal identifiers are transferred during these pack updates.
- **Data Deletion:** Parents and teachers can clear all stored progress and saved words at any time with a single tap via the browser settings or app cache clearance.

---

### 6. Parental & Guardian Rights (DPDP Act 2023)
In full compliance with Section 9 of the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023)** regarding the processing of personal data of children:
- **Right to Review:** Parents and guardians have the right to inspect any data associated with their child's classroom profile.
- **Right to Rectification & Erasure:** Parents may request the immediate deletion or correction of any stored data.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Parents may revoke consent at any time, upon which all localized cookies, tokens, and progress records will be expunged.
- **No Detrimental Processing:** We certify that no data processing is undertaken that could cause harm, discrimination, or psychological distress to any child.

---

### 7. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing
- **No Third-Party Ad Networks:** BhashaSetu does not include Google AdSense, Facebook Pixel, tracking cookies, or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs.
- **Infrastructure Providers:** Cloud hosting (such as FastAPI backend deployments) is managed through secure cloud infrastructure configured with TLS 1.3 encryption in transit. Infrastructure partners process data solely as technical conduits under strict confidentiality terms.

### 8. Security Safeguards
We implement industry-standard safeguards to protect young users:
- **Encrypted Communication:** All client-server communications use HTTPS with modern TLS encryption protocols.
- **Sandboxed Execution:** Student code and interactive learning exercises execute in sandboxed client environments without access to local device file systems.
- **Strict Content Moderation:** Inputs and translated story outputs pass through regex and dictionary safety filters to eliminate inappropriate language before rendering on screen.
