Part I: Terms and Conditions of Use

Guidelines governing the safe and respectful use of the BhashaSetu learning platform

1 Acceptance of Terms & Parental/Guardian Consent

By accessing, browsing, or using BhashaSetu ("the Platform", "we", "us"), including the Web Portal, Android App, and Vernacular AI translation services, you acknowledge and agree to these Terms and Conditions.

  • For Children (Minors under 18): If you are under 18 years of age, you may only use BhashaSetu with the verifiable guidance and consent of a parent, legal guardian, teacher, or authorized school administrator.
  • For Parents, Guardians & Teachers: By permitting a child to use BhashaSetu, you confirm that you have reviewed and approved these Terms and the Privacy Policy on their behalf and assume responsibility for their platform usage.

2 Educational Purpose & Scope

BhashaSetu is an educational technology initiative created to address early-grade learning poverty and language comprehension gaps in low-resource and tribal schools.

  • The platform is provided strictly for educational, non-profit, and pedagogical learning.
  • Our pedagogical methodology translates standard primary curriculum concepts into culturally familiar regional metaphors aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020).

3 Child Safety & Zero-Harassment Rules

To maintain a nurturing, gentle, and strictly positive learning environment for young children:

  • Encouraging Language Only: The platform may only be used for constructive schoolwork, reading practice, and joyful vernacular exploration.
  • Strictly Prohibited: Transmitting offensive, violent, vulgar, discriminatory, or adult content; attempting to extract private user data; or tampering with API endpoints.
  • Automated Safety Filters: Automated text-safety filters and Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) moderation instantly reject and discard inappropriate inputs.

4 AI Pedagogy & Vernacular Accuracy Advisory

BhashaSetu combines rule-based dictionaries, linguist-verified vocabularies, and AI speech models to translate into indigenous languages (such as Santhali, Mundari, Ho, Kurukh, Kharia, Khortha, Nagpuri, Panchpargania, Kurmali, Bengali, and Tamil).

💡
Teacher-Assistive Technology Advisory:

While verified by linguistic experts, automated dialect translations may contain minor colloquial variations. BhashaSetu is designed as a companion to classroom educators, not an autonomous replacement for teachers.

5 Cultural Heritage & Indigenous Language Rights

BhashaSetu recognizes and honors the sovereign cultural and intellectual heritage of indigenous and tribal communities. Indigenous scripts (including Ol Chiki, Warang Citi, and Tolong Siki) and regional folktales belong to their respective heritage communities and are treated with utmost reverence.

6 Student Identifiers & Picture Passwords

To ensure young children are not intimidated by complex passwords, BhashaSetu supports child-friendly logins such as Student First Names and Picture Passwords (e.g., tapping 🐘 + 🥭). These credentials should never contain sensitive government identity identifiers (such as Aadhaar or passport numbers).

7 Offline-First Operation

BhashaSetu is engineered to work reliably in rural classrooms with zero or intermittent internet connectivity. Core dictionaries, pre-cached stories, and speech synthesis operate locally on devices.

Part II: Privacy Policy (DPDP Act 2023 Compliant)

How we safeguard child data in strict accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act

1 Core Privacy Principles

We operate under strict privacy-by-design standards:

  • Zero Commercial Profiling: We do not track children across the web, nor do we construct behavioral ad profiles.
  • Zero Data Monetization: No child data is ever sold, rented, or traded.
  • Data Minimization: We only process the absolute minimum data required to deliver learning lessons.

2 Transparent Data Auditing

Here is an explicit breakdown of what we collect versus what we NEVER collect:

Government ID / Aadhaar ❌ Never Collected We never ask for or accept government identity records. Nowhere
Biometric Facial / Voiceprints ❌ Never Collected No biometric identification or voice fingerprinting. Nowhere
Precise GPS Tracking ❌ Never Collected We do not track physical coordinates or real-time location. Nowhere

3 Voice, Audio & Speech Data Protocol

BhashaSetu includes interactive voice buttons so children can hear and practice native pronunciations:

  • Speech Synthesis (Text-to-Speech): Synthesized locally using on-device Web Audio synthesizers and speech engines. No audio recording is stored.
  • Microphone Input (Speech Practice): When a child speaks into the microphone to practice a word, audio is processed in temporary volatile memory solely for transcription and immediately purged.
  • No Biometric Storage: We do not store, catalog, or analyze voice patterns for biometric speaker identification.

4 Parental & Guardian Rights (Section 9, DPDP Act 2023)

Parents, legal guardians, and school administrators possess sovereign rights regarding their child's learning profile:

  • Right to Review: Inspect any saved words or localized learning records associated with your child.
  • Right to Complete Erasure: Clear all local profile history, picture credentials, and saved stars with a single tap in browser settings or by contacting our team.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke consent at any time without penalty.

5 Zero Third-Party Advertising & Tracking

BhashaSetu contains no third-party ad networks (no Google AdSense, Facebook Pixel, or commercial marketing SDKs). All cloud endpoints operate on secure infrastructure over encrypted TLS 1.3 connections.
