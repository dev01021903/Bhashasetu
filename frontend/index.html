🌿 ABOUT BHASHASETU

About BhashaSetu

Building a bridge between children, culture, and language.

OUR IDEA

Learning begins in the language of home.

Children learn best when they can connect new ideas to the words they hear in their homes. BhashaSetu bridges the gap between indigenous tribal languages and state languages (Hindi & English), empowering young minds to celebrate their cultural roots while excelling in modern education.

Our Vision

A world where no child feels left behind because of their mother tongue, and every indigenous language is celebrated and preserved digitally.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide AI-powered, joyful, and culturally respectful learning tools for primary education in tribal and regional mother tongues.

WHAT WE DO

How BhashaSetu Helps

Simple, fun, and accessible tools designed specifically for children aged 5–10.

📚

🔊

Play & Practise

Grow Together

Collaborative classroom features and community contributions from teachers and parents.

Every Language Has a Story

Indigenous languages carry centuries of wisdom, folklore, and connection to nature. When a child learns in their mother tongue, they carry forward a living legacy of community and identity.
